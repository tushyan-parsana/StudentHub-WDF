Help Desk

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

How do I enroll in a course?

Visit the Course Catalog, choose a course, and click the Add button.

How can I contact my faculty?

Open the course page and use the faculty contact information provided.

Where can I see my assignments?

Navigate to Academics → Assignments from the sidebar.

Still Need Help?

Submit a support request and our Help Desk team will contact you shortly.
